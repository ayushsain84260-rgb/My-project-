PrepTrack

Version 1.5 • Made for focused preparation

PrepTrack is a personal exam-preparation and study-management app designed to help students organize their preparation, track daily progress, manage study plans, monitor their exam countdown, and stay consistent.

Core Capabilities

  • Exam Date
  • Countdown
  • Daily Targets
  • Study Plans
  • Calendar
  • Progress
  • Streaks
  • Focus Timer
  • Prep Card
  • Analytics
  • Custom Theme
  • Data Mgmt

How to Use

Your quick start guide to Elite PrepTrack.

1

Get Started

Enter your operational alias, select your target domains (exams), and set your exam date to initialize your workspace.

2

Exam Countdown

The dashboard automatically calculates and prominently displays the exact days remaining until your target exam date.

3

Dashboard & Focus

Your command center. View daily progress, study streak, and daily motivation. Start a focus session by swiping left on a target and pressing Play.

4

Daily Targets

Add new study modules. Tap the checkbox to complete them. Swipe left on a row to reveal Focus Timer and Delete actions.

5

Study Plans

Create macro strategies with specific start and end dates to successfully map out your long-term execution.

6

Calendar

Navigate the timeline to review past performance, check historical streaks, and schedule future study operations directly on specific dates.

7

Progress & Analytics

Monitor lifetime completion percentages, view daily/weekly streaks, and visualize your recent efforts with premium charts in the Profile section.

8

Club & Members

The multiplayer Syndicate area is currently under construction, but will allow squad progression in the future.

9

Personal Prep Card

View, seamlessly download, or share a beautiful, personalized summary card of your current prep status directly from your Profile.

10

Settings & Appearance

Edit your profile alias, active exams, or deadline. Customize your app with Dark Mode and dynamic accent colors in Appearance.

11

Data & Backup

Your data is securely saved locally on your device. Use the System Reset option to completely erase local data if a fresh start is needed.
